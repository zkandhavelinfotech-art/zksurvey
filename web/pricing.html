Questions

Do respondents need an account? No. They open your link and answer.

Can I post directly to Instagram or Facebook? Not yet. Today you download the poster and copy the caption (or use the share button where the platform has one). Direct publishing and Meta Ads sync arrive in Phase 8.

Where is my data? In a PostgreSQL database on the ZKSurvey server. You can export or delete it from Settings at any time.
