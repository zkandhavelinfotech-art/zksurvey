ZK Growth360 — Research to Scale

The end-to-end programme ZKSurvey supports for a new-market launch such as Milro Dishwash in Coimbatore.

1 · Research

Survey 200–500 households. Category-first, unaided questions; do not name the brand or explain the product early.

2 · Segment

A existing liquid users · B bar users · C price-conscious families · D convenience-focused households. See Segments.

3 · Position

Match the strongest pain point and message to each segment.

4 · Trial

Trial packs through WhatsApp, e-commerce and nearby supermarkets; capture consented leads.

5 · Launch

Tagged campaigns with QR and links. See Campaigns.

6 · Optimise & Scale

Track leads and conversion, repeat and referral, then expand to new segments and cities via the Strategy Builder.

“We will use ZK Growth360 to research the Coimbatore market, identify high-potential customer segments, validate product acceptance, and build a go-to-market launch strategy.”
How ZKSurvey creates business value: the survey is not treated as the end product. It is the evidence and permission layer that feeds segmentation, positioning, testing, lead capture, launch campaigns and continuous optimisation.
