  • You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and two-factor authentication tokens.

2. Privacy Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

We comply fully with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. We respect the privacy of both our account holders and survey respondents (Data Principals).

2.1 Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, work email address, company name, GSTIN (optional), and encrypted password credentials (scrypt-hashed).
  • Billing & Payment Data: Transaction IDs, UPI Reference Numbers (UTR), Order IDs, and GST-compliant invoice records. We do not store raw card numbers or banking PINs.
  • Survey Responses: Survey inputs, demographic choices, ratings, and optional contact leads. IP addresses are pseudonymized/hashed and used strictly for anti-bot rate limiting.

2.2 Rights of Data Principals

Under the DPDP Act 2023, individuals whose data is processed hold unambiguous legal rights:

  • Right to Accessible Notice & Consent: Every survey published displays a clear consent notice. The timestamp, IP hash, and exact consent text version are stored with each response record.
  • Right to Erasure & Correction: Account holders can export all workspace surveys, responses, and audit logs with one click from Settings → Export Data or permanently delete their account and associated responses.
  • Right of Withdrawal: Respondents may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the survey owner or our Data Grievance Officer.

3. Data Fiduciary and Data Processor Distinction

To ensure total clarity regarding legal compliance and liability under Indian and international privacy statutes:

Survey Creator as Data Fiduciary: When you design and publish a survey to gather public or customer responses, you act as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act. You define the purpose of collection, verify lawful basis, and are responsible for responding to respondent data deletion requests.

ZKSurvey as Data Processor: ZKSurvey acts purely as a Data Processor providing infrastructure, encryption, database storage, and analytical dashboards. We process survey responses solely in accordance with your instructions and do not sell, license, or monetize respondent data.

4. Subscriptions, Payments & UPI Refund Policy

4.1 Payment Rails & Verification

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM, Paytm): Users can upgrade instantly by scanning the dynamic UPI QR code or transferring to our verified VPA/UPI ID. Verification is completed via 12-digit Bank Reference Number (UTR) submission.
  • Razorpay: Credit/Debit cards, NetBanking, and corporate corporate cards are processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified Razorpay payment gateways.
  • Taxes: Indian clients will be invoiced with applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST 18% under SAC 998315). Valid GSTIN holders will receive GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) compliant tax invoices.

4.2 Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • 7-Day Trial Satisfaction Guarantee: If you upgrade to a paid tier (Starter or Growth) and find that ZKSurvey does not fulfill your business requirements, you may request a 100% refund within 7 calendar days of your initial payment, provided response consumption is under 10% of your plan quota.
  • Refund Process: Approved refunds will be credited directly back to the original source account (UPI VPA or bank account) within 5–7 business days.
  • Plan Downgrades: You may cancel or downgrade your subscription at any time; your premium tier capabilities will remain active until the end of your prepaid billing cycle.
